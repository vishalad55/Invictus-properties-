## Why the registration page is worth ten minutes

Everything else a buyer is shown is prepared by the seller. The registration
filing is also prepared by the seller, but it is filed with a regulator, it is
public, and it can be compared against the brochure. That comparison is the whole
exercise.

## Step one: find the project, not the advertisement

Karnataka, with the current URL. Describe how to search by project name,
promoter name and registration number, as the portal actually works today.

A project marketed under a name that does not appear on the portal is the first
thing to resolve. The marketing name and the registered name are often different.
Ask for the registration number rather than searching by the name on the hoarding.

## Step two: check the registration itself

- That a registration exists for this project and this phase. {{VERIFY}} how
  phases are registered and displayed.
- That it is current rather than lapsed or extended. {{VERIFY}} how validity and
  any extension appear on the page.
- That the promoter named matches the entity you are contracting with. A group
  name on a brochure is not the same as the registered promoter entity.

{{VERIFY}} Describe what the page shows about the promoter's other registered
projects and any complaints or orders, as the portal presents it.

## Step three: match the filing against the brochure

Take the brochure and the filing side by side. Check each of the following
appears in both, and matches.

- The survey numbers and the extent of land.
- The approvals and sanctioned plan attached to the filing.
- The unit configurations, sizes and the basis on which area is stated.
  {{VERIFY}} the defined area terms used in the current law and state them exactly.
- The amenities committed in the filing, as against the ones shown in renders.
- The declared completion date.

Where the brochure claims something the filing does not contain, the claim is not
backed by the filing. That is not automatically dishonest, but it is a question,
and it should be answered in writing.

## Step four: the declared date

The completion date on the filing is the promoter's own declaration to the
regulator. It is the date to plan around, rather than the one used in a sales
conversation.

{{VERIFY}} State what the law provides where that date is missed, what a buyer's
remedies are, and how extensions work. Do not state any figure, interest rate or
limitation period without checking the current provision.

## What this means for a developer marketing a project

Advertised particulars have to match the registration. This is the discipline
behind how we write project marketing: if a particular is not in the filing, it
does not go in the ad, and a date is never implied in creative that the filing
does not carry.
